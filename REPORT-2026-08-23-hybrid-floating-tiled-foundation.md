# Typhon Hybrid Floating/Tiled Foundation

Date: 2026-08-23

Status: implemented in the existing working tree; no commit created.

## Architecture retained

The workspace model remains orthogonal:

- `WorkspaceLocation::Regular(WorkspaceId)` and `WorkspaceLocation::Special(SpecialWorkspaceId)` remain canonical WM membership.
- `LayoutMembership::Floating` and `LayoutMembership::Tiled` remain an independent axis.
- `ActiveSceneSelection` and `ActiveSceneView` remain derived presentation state.
- `SceneWorkOwner` is typed as `Global` or `Location(WorkspaceLocation)` and is rebuilt from canonical surface/window relationships.

Auxiliary application-tree surfaces resolve visibility and scene-work ownership through their canonical managed root. Output-owned/global work remains independent. Workspace membership is migrated through the existing relationship reconciliation path when XDG parentage or X11 transient relationships change, followed by a scene-work index rebuild. No numeric `workspace()` accessor or Special-to-active-Regular fallback remains.

## Preflight scene-transition closure

Workspace and Special transitions now use a transition-active guard around the complete scene mutation. The flow is:

1. capture the old scene selection;
2. update the manager and derive the new selection;
3. identify windows leaving the visible scene;
4. defer pointer refreshes;
5. terminate interactions, constraints, implicit grabs, held presses, and popup grabs owned by departing roots;
6. rebuild `ActiveSceneView` and scene-work bookkeeping;
7. repair keyboard/window focus and perform one canonical pointer refresh against the final scene;
8. commit the final render generation.

Interaction-terminal refresh calls are harmlessly deferred while this guard is active, preventing hidden Special surfaces from being hit-tested during cleanup and preventing duplicate crossing sequences. Regular-window interactions are not cancelled when an unrelated Special window closes.

## Fullscreen/native-frame regression

`compositor::tests::windows::special_workspace_visibility_controls_fullscreen_native_frame_solitude` exercises the real controllable compositor server:

- a Regular fullscreen root remains the fullscreen owner;
- a hidden Special window permits solitary native-frame culling;
- opening Special keeps its root in native-frame renderables;
- `solitary_tree_active` becomes false;
- Direct Scanout is rejected with `OverlayVisible`;
- no configure serial is generated by the scene transition;
- the close transition restores the Regular fullscreen focus and performs one instrumented hit-test refresh.

## Floating/Tiled foundation

`Super+V` toggles the focused managed window’s `LayoutMembership` without recreating its client or changing `WorkspaceLocation`.

Floating-to-Tiled captures the current compositor floating `WindowGeometry` (placement and size) in the desktop-window state. Tiled-to-Floating restores that geometry through the existing placement/visual geometry authority. X11 uses the existing backend configure/state path; XDG does not introduce a new configure pipeline. `ToplevelVisualGeometry`, resize flow ownership, and `MAX_IN_FLIGHT_RESIZE_CONFIGURES` were not redesigned.

Tiled mode is policy-only in this phase: there is no Dwindle tree, placement scan, split ratio, gap, timer, thread, lock, or polling loop. Therefore the foundation adds zero per-frame layout scans and has negligible idle cost until a future layout authority is introduced.

`WindowChromePolicy` is derived solely from `LayoutMembership` (`Full` for Floating and `Minimal` for Tiled). No visual redesign was made, and the policy does not inspect protocol, application, workspace, or Special state.

## Coverage added

- Regular Floating↔Tiled transitions preserve identity, focus, location, and saved geometry.
- Special Floating→Tiled remains valid without changing Special membership or the active Regular workspace.
- Auxiliary scene-work ownership migrates when its canonical parent changes and the auxiliary retains no independent membership.
- Special close during resize and move exercises terminal interaction cleanup; unrelated Regular interaction survives.
- Native input verifies exact Super+V, press-only behavior, disabled repeat, shortcut inhibition, and no leaked key event.
- Fullscreen/Special coverage validates native-frame visibility, solitude, Direct Scanout rejection, focus restoration, and no configure generation.
